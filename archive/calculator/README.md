# Archived : the calculator exercise spine

Exercises 2 to 11 of PRG400 used to grow a command-line calculator, one module
at a time. They were replaced by the Wordle spine in October 2026, because the
calculator gave the later modules (files, a database, classes) no natural job
and the exercises dictated most of their code.

The exercise text and the reference solutions are kept here for trainers who
want them. Nothing in this folder is built, linted for the deck, or shipped in
the student bundle.
